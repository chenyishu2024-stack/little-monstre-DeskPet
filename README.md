## 怎么玩

![主界面](screenshots/main.png)

**主界面**
- 右边便签上有 4 个提醒（休息 / 喝水 / 远眺护眼 / 自定义），点圆圈开关，名字和间隔都能直接改。默认只开"每 2 小时休息一下"。
- 左边的时钟跟着系统时间走。**拖动时针或分针**就能设闹钟，气泡里显示还剩多久，表盘上的小铃铛标出闹钟位置；双击倒计时数字可以取消。
- 点右上角"–"，主界面收起，小怪兽跑到桌面底部陪你。

**桌面上的小怪兽**
- 单击：摸摸头
- 双击：打开主界面
- 右键：菜单（打开主界面 / 切换语言 / 退出）
- 按住拖动：把它拎起来，它会跟着晃；松手会掉回桌面底部
- 平时它会自己溜达、发呆、偶尔扭扭屁股；你很久不碰电脑，它会睡着
- 时间到了，它会跳出来"说话"提醒你，点它一下表示知道了

**语言**：中文系统自动显示中文，其他系统显示英文；右键菜单里可以随时切换。

## English

**littleMonstre_deskPet** is a tiny pixel monster that lives at the bottom of your desktop. It reminds you to rest, drink water and look away from the screen, and works as an alarm clock too. Available in English and Chinese.

### Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/chenyishu2024-stack/little-monstre-DeskPet/releases/latest):

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) | `littleMonstre_deskPet-Setup-x.x.x.exe` |
| Mac with Apple chip (M1/M2/M3…) | `littleMonstre_deskPet-x.x.x-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel chip | `littleMonstre_deskPet-x.x.x.dmg` |

The app is not code-signed yet, so your system will warn you the first time:

- **Windows**: "Windows protected your PC" → click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- **Mac**: drag the app into Applications, then **right-click** it → **Open** → **Open**.

### How to play

- **Main window**: 4 reminders (Rest / Water / Eye break / Custom) — click a circle to turn one on, and edit names and intervals directly. **Drag the clock hands** to set an alarm; a little bell marks it on the dial, and the bubble shows the time left (double-click it to cancel). Click "–" to shrink into the desktop pet.
- **Desktop pet**: click to pat its head · double-click to open the main window · right-click for the menu (language / quit) · drag to pick it up (it swings!) and let go to drop it. It wanders, naps when you're away, and pops up to "talk" when it's time — click it to dismiss.
- **Language**: follows your system language; switch anytime from the right-click menu.
